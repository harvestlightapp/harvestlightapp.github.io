FAQs

Do I need to create an account?

No. HarvestLight doesn't have a login or signup of any kind. If you're signed in to iCloud on your device, your dates and alarms sync privately across your own devices through your own iCloud account. There's nothing to set up beyond that.

How do the alarms work?

When you set up shooting hours, an alarm is created for the start and end times. You can adjust these alarms if needed. For example, if you plan to hunt on your way back to the truck, you might set the end time alarm 20 minutes early. The alarms use the vibration pattern that you pick. All of your data stays on your device and your iCloud account (if you're signed it). We don't collect or save anything you set up.

Do you set hunting dates and shooting hours automatically based on my location?

Nope! We're keeping it simple: you enter this info manually. You're responsible for knowing and adhering to hunting regulations, including legal shooting hours.

Why should I use HarvestLight instead of the built-in alarms app?

Alarms need to be dismissed by pressing a button, which can mess up your hunt. HarvestLight vibrates in your pocket, telling you that shooting hours have started or ended without moving a muscle. You don't have to check your watch or press a button on your phone.

Why do I get popups when the alarm goes off?

As it turns out, there's no way around showing these popups when sending a vibration. We wish there were! If Apple ever lets us change this, we will.

Do I have to use seasons?

Seasons (like "Goose season") are optional, but can be a helpful way to organize your hunting dates and alarms. A season only affects how dates are organized and displayed, not how alarms work. Any date can be left in "No season" and everything still works exactly the same.

What happens to past dates?

Once a date has passed, it moves to "Past dates" automatically. You can view and edit past dates there. For example, you might keep last year's deer season dates and adjust the dates and times for the current year, rather than starting over.

How do I share my dates with someone else?

Pick the season or individual dates that you want to send, then click the share button. You can share via text, AirDrop, email, or whatever you choose. The recipient receives a link that opens the HarvestLight app (if it's already installed) and imports your hunting dates and alarms. Note that the recipient must have an iPhone; HarvestLight isn't available for Android.

Can I use this app on iPad?

You can use the app on iPad, but you won't receive iPad alerts. iPad doesn't have a vibration function, so it can't receive silent alarms. But it can be very handy to set up your alarms using your iPad's bigger screen. If your iPad and iPhone are signed in to the same iCloud account, they will automatically be synced. If not, you can send your alarms to your iPhone from the share button using AirDrop, text, or email.

Is my data private?

Yes. HarvestLight has no analytics, no ads, and no third-party tracking of any kind. Your dates and alarms live in your own private iCloud account, and the only time any of it leaves your device is if you deliberately share a link with someone. See the full privacy policy for details.

An alarm didn't go off — what should I check?

  • Make sure notifications are allowed for HarvestLight in Settings → Notifications.
  • Make sure your phone is on silent.
  • Make sure the alarm's start/end toggle is actually on for that date, and that the date is correct.
  • Avoid force-quitting the app from the app switcher; it isn't necessary and background refresh works best when the app hasn't been manually closed.
